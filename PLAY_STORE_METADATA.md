## 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
Check before you click. Protect yourself from modern cyber fraud with ScamShield.

Every day, millions of people receive deceptive text messages, fake parcel delivery alerts, urgent banking warnings, and spoofed links designed to steal money and login credentials. ScamShield helps you evaluate suspicious communications before you respond, click, or send money.

🛡️ MULTI-MODAL SCAM & PHISHING CHECKER

• CHECK MESSAGES: Paste suspicious SMS alerts, emails, WhatsApp messages, Telegram chats, fake job offers, and crypto investment promises.
• CHECK LINKS & URLS: Inspect deceptive websites, hidden redirects, URL shorteners, punycode lookalike domains, and brand typosquatting.
• SCAN SCREENSHOTS: Import screenshots with on-device text recognition. Review and edit extracted text before analyzing to ensure complete accuracy.
• QR CODE INSPECTOR: Point your camera at any QR code to safely inspect its hidden destination URL and domain before opening it in your browser.
• SHARE SHEET INTEGRATION: Share any text, link, or image directly from your messaging apps into ScamShield with a single tap.

🔍 EXPLAINABLE RISK ASSESSMENT — NO BLACK BOXES

ScamShield doesn't just give you a vague number. Every security assessment breaks down:
✓ Calibrated Risk Score (0 to 100) & Risk Category
✓ Specific Red Flags Detected (Urgency, Impersonation, Credential Theft, Gift Card Demands)
✓ Severity level for each indicator (Critical, Warning, Informational)
✓ Contextual Recommended Next Actions (e.g., "Do not click", "Verify sender independently")
✓ Human Verification Reminders highlighting what automated tools cannot verify

🔒 PRIVACY-FIRST & ZERO INVASIVE PERMISSIONS

ScamShield is engineered with strict Data Minimization:
• NO BACKGROUND HARVESTING: We never request permissions to read your SMS, contacts, call logs, or notifications.
• USER-INITIATED ONLY: The app only evaluates content that you intentionally paste or share.
• LOCAL-FIRST PROCESSING: Core heuristic security checks run 100% on your device without transmitting data over the internet.
• OFFLINE MODE: Enable "Offline Analysis Only" in Settings for complete zero-network security analysis.
• FULL DATA CONTROL: Disable scan history or erase all local data permanently anytime with one tap.

📚 SCAM PREVENTION KNOWLEDGE CENTER

Learn to spot the warning signs of modern cyber attacks:
• Phishing & Spear-Phishing
• Fake Parcel Delivery Surcharges
• Work-From-Home & Job Scams
• Cryptocurrency & High-Yield Investment Fraud
• Romance & Catfishing Scams
• Bank & Government Impersonation
• Tech Support Pop-up Alerts
• Quishing (Malicious QR Codes)
• Account Takeover & 2FA Code Theft

⚠️ RESPONSIBLE SECURITY NOTICE:
ScamShield provides risk assessments based on known threat heuristics and anomaly detection. Fraud tactics constantly evolve. ScamShield cannot guarantee that any content is 100% safe. Always remain vigilant and verify institutions through independent channels.
```

## 5. Google Play Data Safety Declaration

| Data Safety Question | Answer | Details / Justification |
|---|---|---|
| **Does the app collect or share user data?** | **No** (MVP) | User content analyzed locally. No personal data shared with third parties. |
| **Is data encrypted in transit?** | **Yes** | When optional online AI analysis is enabled, all requests use HTTPS TLS 1.3. |
| **Does the app request READ_SMS?** | **No** | Zero background SMS access. Users intentionally paste text. |
| **Does the app request READ_CALL_LOG?** | **No** | Zero call log access. |
| **Does the app request READ_CONTACTS?** | **No** | Zero contact access. |
| **Can users request data deletion?** | **Yes** | Settings → "Delete All Local Data" permanently erases all scans with confirmation. |
| **Target Audience** | **Everyone (Ages 13+)** | Standard consumer cybersecurity tool. |
